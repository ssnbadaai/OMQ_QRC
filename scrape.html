Collect

The first two are structured data the site already publishes for machines: fast, clean, and a handful of requests. Page text is one request per address, so a site of a few thousand pages takes hours at a polite pace. Start without it and see what the other two return.

Signed-in content, and robots.txt

Sent with each request so member-only pages return their real content. Use your own account, for content you are allowed to read. It is held in this tab only — never saved, never logged, gone when you close the page.

Turning this off means fetching paths the site has asked crawlers to leave alone. Only do that on a site you run, or with its owner's permission.
